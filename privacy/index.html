Privacy

Lanternway · iPhone · November 8, 2026

Lanternway is a focus app for studying. We built it to collect as little as possible. Study progress stays on your device. Anonymous usage analytics go to TelemetryDeck. There is no advertising SDK and no tracking across apps.

What stays on your device

Your journeys and study log, subjects, story progress, wardrobe, streaks and settings are stored only on your device (and in your device backups). We do not have a server that receives them.

What we collect: anonymous usage analytics

We use TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how the app is used — for example, that a 25-minute journey was completed, that the Stats screen was opened, or that onboarding was finished.

Signals are tied to a random identifier created on your device and hashed before sending. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple Account, advertising identifier or device identifiers.

We never send names, free text, what you study in your own words, contacts, location or anything typed. Analytics are not used for advertising or tracking across apps or websites. “Delete my data” in Settings resets this identifier.

Screen Time

If you choose apps to keep dark during a journey, Apple’s Screen Time framework gives Lanternway opaque tokens. We cannot see which apps or websites you picked, and they never leave your device.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive only whether a purchase is active, never payment details.

Notifications

Reminders and journey notifications are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from our servers.

Children and teens

Lanternway is designed for students, including teens. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Age information provided by Apple (age range) is used only to adapt features and is not stored by us.

Your choices

Turn off notifications or Screen Time at any time in iOS Settings. Delete my data (Settings → Help & privacy) erases everything Lanternway stores on your device.
