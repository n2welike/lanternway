Short answers

The journey stopped when I left the app

That is expected after a short grace. Leaving for a real emergency does not break the streak. Giving up only costs that session — hours already walked stay on the map.

Screen Time / blocked apps

Lanternway only receives opaque tokens from Apple. We cannot see which apps you picked. You can change or remove the shield in iOS Settings at any time.

Purchases

Handled by Apple. Restore purchases from Settings if you reinstall. Focus minutes, fog and streak days are never sold.

Delete my data

Settings → Help & privacy. That erases what Lanternway stores on the device and resets the anonymous analytics identifier.
